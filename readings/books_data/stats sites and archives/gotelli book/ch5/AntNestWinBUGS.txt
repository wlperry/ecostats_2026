# Enter prior estimate of among-group variance s0 into the formula for precision (tau)

# nu is the "strength of belief", expressed as an integer "degrees of freedom"
# variance =1/precision.
# An uninformative prior on precision would be dgamma(0.0001, 0.0001)
# hence in an uninformative prior, precision is small and variance is very large.
#
# Example: estimate prior variance as 1 with modest belief (nu = 2)
