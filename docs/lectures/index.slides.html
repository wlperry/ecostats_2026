Every lecture below pulls its title, topic, and description straight from the .qmd file’s YAML frontmatter — nothing here is hand-typed. Ordering comes from the order: field, which the sync_order_from_folder_names.R pre-render hook fills in automatically from each folder’s WW_MM_ number prefix. To reorder or move a lecture to another week, just change the two leading numbers on its folder name — the frontmatter and this page update themselves on the next render.
